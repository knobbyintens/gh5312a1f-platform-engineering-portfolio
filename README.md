# platform-engineering-portfolio

Every internal engineering project lives in its own directory under `projects/`.

- Ingested research reports are deposited as archival project directories by the
  research intake bot; those directories contain the submitted manuscript and
  notes only.
- Engineering projects are named after the technique or subsystem they build and
  are maintained by the owning team.
- The adoption review registry is kept in `projects/platform-adoption-registry`.
